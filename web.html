Península de Zamboanga: ¡Celebra con el Vida!

Zamboanga Peninsula is also known as "Asia's Latin City" due to its Spanish-creole language, Chavacano.

The name "Zamboanga" comes from the Malay word jambangan, which means "garden of flowers" or "flowers in a vase".

It is also known as the "Sardines Capital of the Philippines" because it supplies much of the country's processed fish.
